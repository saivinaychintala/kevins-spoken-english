What is the difference between American Accent IPA Training and the 10-month Spoken English course?

The Accent Intensive is a focused, roughly one-month program: IPA phonetics in Telugu, mouth positioning, Flap T, American vowels, and MTI reduction — so you sound clearer on US client calls. Kevin’s Complete Spoken English Course is a 10-month language system: grammar, sentence formation, translation, reading, comprehension, vocabulary, listening, writing, and speaking projects, with daily homework. Choose Accent if pronunciation is the blocker. Choose the 10-month course if you need a full foundation. Many students do both.

I already watched Kevin’s Spoken English on YouTube. Why should I enroll?

YouTube teaches the idea. Enrollment gives you the sequenced classroom, lifetime replay, and daily homework that Venkat personally corrects on Telegram. Watching is optional education. Submission is what changes your speech.

Will this help me with US clients and interviews?

It is built for Telugu speakers who must be understood in professional English — including US client calls, accent-sensitive roles, and interviews. IPA targets clarity. The 10-month course targets accuracy and fluency.
