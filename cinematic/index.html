Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the Accent Intensive and the 10-month course?

The Accent Intensive focuses on phonetics, mouth mechanics, and MTI reduction for US client calls. The 10-month course builds overall language confidence, grammar, and daily sentence structure.

How does Telegram audio homework work?

After each lesson, you record a short voice note of the assigned drill and send it on Telegram. Venkat listens and gives personal corrections. Homework goes to @aviperon.

Is this suitable for Telugu-medium students?

Yes. Lessons and IPA are explained in Telugu so the method is usable from a Telugu-medium school background.
